Sources and dosing scope

Source review: September 23, 2026. Every medication row links directly to its U.S. prescribing information, professional guideline, or supporting study. Ranges were aligned to those sources during this review; off-label uses are identified in the medication name.

How weight bases and references were chosen

Product labeling is the primary source whenever it provides the displayed indication and range. Rows marked with an asterisk use an obesity-focused scalar supported by a systematic review of perioperative dosing in adults with obesity ↗ or Association of Anaesthetists guidance ↗; this scalar may differ from product labeling and is not a substitute for titration, neuromuscular monitoring, or local policy. Remifentanil follows its label-specific obesity and geriatric adjustments. Local-anesthetic rows show calculated maxima capped at the cited adult maximum total dose. The esmolol laryngoscopy/intubation entry remains off-label and links to its supporting studies.
